💡 What This Means

Packing Efficiency measures how well your content fills TAR files before Oak creates a new generation. Oak's maximum TAR file size is 256 MB.

  • <10% 🔴 Many small TAR files (inefficient, high fragmentation tax)
  • 10-50% 🟡 Moderate efficiency (typical during growth phase)
  • >50% 🟢 Good efficiency (well-packed, lower tax)

Why It Matters: Low packing efficiency indicates fragmentation, which triggers higher storage tax multipliers and may require compaction. Aim for TAR files close to 256 MB for optimal storage economics.
